# BSA/AML Examination Manual — Customer Identification Program (CIP)
# Source: FFIEC BSA/AML Examination Manual, Section: Customer Identification Program
# Simplified extract for AegisLang pipeline evaluation

## 1. CIP Program Requirements

Each bank must implement a written Customer Identification Program appropriate for its size and type of business.
The CIP must be incorporated into the bank's BSA/AML compliance program.
The board of directors must approve the CIP.

## 2. Identity Verification Procedures

Banks must verify the identity of each customer using documentary or non-documentary methods.
Documentary verification methods include reviewing an unexpired government-issued identification document bearing a photograph.
Non-documentary methods may include contacting the customer, comparing information with consumer reporting agencies, or checking references.

## 3. Recordkeeping Requirements

Banks must retain records of the identifying information obtained from customers for five years after the account is closed.
Records shall include a description of any document relied upon for identity verification, including the type of document, identification number, place of issuance, and date of issuance or expiration.
Institutions must retain records of the methods used and results of any non-documentary verification measures.

## 4. Government Lists

Banks must determine whether a customer appears on any list of known or suspected terrorists or terrorist organizations.
Institutions shall check customer names against the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) Specially Designated Nationals list.
Staff must not open accounts for individuals or entities identified on government sanctions lists.

## 5. Customer Notice

Banks must provide adequate notice to customers that the institution is requesting information to verify their identity.
The notice must be provided before or at the time of account opening.
Institutions may satisfy this requirement through posting a notice in the lobby or on the website.

## 6. Reliance on Other Financial Institutions

A bank may rely on another financial institution to perform CIP procedures if certain conditions are met.
The relied-upon institution must be subject to a rule implementing the BSA/AML compliance program requirements.
Banks must enter into a written agreement with the other institution specifying the delegated CIP procedures.
